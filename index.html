01 About

I own production software end to end — architecture, code, deployment, and the debugging that keeps it running.

For the past two years I built and operated a multi-tenant B2B SaaS platform at WJS Digital Innovations — designing service boundaries, securing tenant data, and shipping features from the first requirement to production. In 2026 I joined ZioNet, contributing backend services to iTeach, a cloud-native .NET learning platform.

I like systems that are boring in the best way: predictable under load, easy to reason about, and honest about failure. That is also how I use AI — as a fast collaborator whose output is reviewed, tested and validated before it ships.

I work closely with product owners and teammates, review code, and enjoy mentoring. I led CRM testing and quality validation with 50+ Google developers through a Technion QA partnership, and I have taught programming and math to students for years.
